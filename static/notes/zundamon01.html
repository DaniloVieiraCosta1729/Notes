Fisrt, let's translate the title of the video.

We begin with the word 関数 - かんすう, which means "function". The first kanji reminds me of 雷門 - らいもん from Inazuma Eleven, and 雷門 reminds me of 雷影 - らいかげ from Naruto. Only with this relation, we can see that 雷 has something to do with lightning, and, in fact, it does means lightning. For me it is even more easy to remember because lightning in portuguese is, maybe coincidentally, "raio".

Going back to the title: it uses kanjis that I saw in words like 雷門, like I said, but also in words like 数学 which means math. Basically combining 関 that can have many meanings, like gate, relation between data, checkpoint and border -- kansai, kanto are name of places that means something related to border -- and 数 is related to number, math etc.

The particle: を, is just saying the 関数 is the object of the sentence. 

The word 因数 - いんすう means "factor" and its parts are way more easy to remember than 関. 因 means origin, motive etc. so it the meaning is very straightforward. 

Now 分解しよう: it means "let's break it down". 

So the whole phrase means let's breaking down the factorization of functions. 
In school we learned how to factorize expression, that is basically means tranform the sum in product. In highschool we are presented to the Algebra fundamental theorem, which says that any polynomial with complex coeficient -- C[x]\mathbb{C} [x]C[x] -- has, at least, one complex root -- or zero, I'm not sure if English uses the term root to talking about a zero of a function --. 

The Weierstrass theorem is a kind of generalization of the factorization, but for "entire functions" -- I'll explain what that means soon--. An function is holomorphic in a point $z$ of $\mathbb{C}$ if it is derivible at this point and in it's neighborhood -- neighborhood in the sense of Analysis, like, given an $r \in \mathbb{R}$, $r > 0$ all the points on the surface of a sphere of radius $r$ and center at the point where the function is holomorphic, is the neighborhood of this point--. An "entire function" is a complex function that is holomorphic in any point of $\mathbb{C}$.
